What changes when you add LiveAsk?

Your website contains the information. LiveAsk gives visitors an easier way to access it.

With

LiveAsk helps guide the experience.

Without LiveAsk

Visitors use the website in the usual way.

Here's what changes for your visitors…

LiveAsk becomes a primary guide.

Positioned prominently beneath your banner and menu, it gives visitors an immediate place to ask about your business, products or services.

Visitors are left to find their own way.

They have to work out where to go, what to read and whether they've found the right answer.

Menus can start conversations.

Selected menu items can tell LiveAsk what the visitor wants and immediately begin the relevant conversation.

Menus send visitors searching.

They move from page to page trying to piece together the answer — and some simply give up.

“Contact Us” becomes intelligent qualification.

LiveAsk quickly confirms primary contact info such as the mobile number, which it confirms using SMS verification.

“Contact Us” usually just pops a form.

The business may only discover an incorrect number when someone tries to make contact.

LiveAsk can move the conversation naturally towards collecting contact details.

If your business sells a product or service, LiveAsk can be trained to be a front line salesperson — qualifying customer needs.

The website has to wait for the visitor to act.

The content can encourage contact, but there's no active engagement to facilitate it.

Conversational intelligence → Governed business truth → Useful, approved action

Here's what makes LiveAsk fundamentally different…

LiveAsk adapts the conversation.

Visitors can ask anything in their own words — and LiveAsk can generate useful choices in the moment to make the next step easier. Choices are shortcuts, not rails.

Chatbots often follow predetermined paths.

Choices such as Sales, Support, Billing or Other are usually designed in advance rather than generated from the conversation.

Generative AI. Governed business knowledge.

LiveAsk responds naturally to the conversation while defined business truth and boundaries keep it grounded.

General-purpose AI can often improvise.

Without business-specific governance, a plausible answer may not be the right answer for your business.

LiveAsk guides what happens next.

It can answer, narrow what the visitor needs, show relevant content or move naturally towards an approved action.

The visitor has to find their own next step.

Information can answer the question, but the visitor must still work out what to do next.

Your business can receive a more complete enquiry.

LiveAsk can hand over who the visitor is, verified contact details and useful context about what they actually want.

Your business receives only the details provided.

The usefulness of the enquiry depends on what the visitor was asked — and chose — to provide.

More LiveAsk enhancements for your website

Three ways LiveAsk turns your website into something visitors can actually talk to…

QuickStarts

Bring your site content alive for visitors.

Add them to help visitors in the right context:

As conversation starters in a section, to help visitors get better insights

As prompts where your content matters most

“Help me choose the right service” “I’m not sure which option suits me”

As “AI Answered FAQs”

Instead of just canned preset FAQs, enable your visitors to more fully engage:

QuickAsk

Some things need explaining — but shouldn’t need another paragraph.

Put a QuickAsk beside a term, feature, price, product or anything visitors might want explained. One click tells LiveAsk exactly what they’re looking at and starts an AI-powered explanation.

No extra searching or navigating for your visitors — they just ask about what’s in front of them.

Try it above or below.

LiveAsk CTA Wizard

What if you don’t know where your website needs a QuickAsk?

CTA Wizard analyses your website and identifies moments where a visitor may hesitate, need clarification or benefit from a conversation — then recommends where QuickStarts and QuickAsks could help.
