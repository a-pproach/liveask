LiveAsk Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

LiveAsk respects the privacy of people who visit our website, use LiveAsk, try a LiveAsk demonstration, or deal with us as a customer or Partner.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and disclosed when you interact with the LiveAsk website, LiveAsk conversations and demonstrations, or our services.

1. Who we are

LiveAsk is operated by Christopher David Carroll (ABN 81 716 061 314) trading as LiveAsk, in Queensland, Australia.

LiveAsk is an AI-powered website interaction and enquiry system. It uses artificial intelligence to answer visitor questions by text or voice and may help visitors identify a next step and, where configured, make an enquiry or contact request. You are talking to an AI, not a person, unless you are connected to a live call.

To contact us about privacy, see section 17.

2. Whose information this covers

  • Visitors to the LiveAsk website and to websites that use LiveAsk.
  • People who try a demonstration, including a person for whom a demonstration was prepared.
  • Customers and Partners who buy or sell LiveAsk services, and their contacts.

Websites that use LiveAsk. When a business uses LiveAsk on its own website, LiveAsk handles visitor information on that business's behalf and passes enquiries to it. That business decides what to do with an enquiry it receives and is responsible for its own use of that information, including its own privacy notice.

3. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, LiveAsk or a demonstration, information we receive may include:

  • what you type into a LiveAsk conversation;
  • what you say in a LiveAsk voice conversation, which is processed live and not kept as audio (see section 5), and the text transcript of it;
  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • the business or organisation you mention, the nature of your enquiry or service interest, and contact preferences or requested contact times;
  • verification codes and the records needed to check them;
  • technical and session information needed to operate and secure the service, such as a random session identifier, the page you were on and limited request details;
  • for customers and Partners, billing contacts, order and payment references, and settlement details; and
  • anything else you choose to give us.

Please do not give sensitive personal information through LiveAsk unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

4. LiveAsk conversations (text)

Text conversations are processed by LiveAsk systems and sent to third-party providers needed to produce a response, currently Anthropic as our AI model provider for text answers. If we change providers, this policy will be updated.

Conversation history may be sent to the provider during a session so that LiveAsk can keep the conversation in context.

Conversation records, including transcripts, are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. LiveAsk's operator may also receive copies of conversations by email for monitoring, quality and safety, and keeps those copies for as long as needed for those purposes.

When a conversation becomes an enquiry or lead, what the visitor supplied and the conversation transcript may be included in a notification email to the recipient named in section 8.

AI-generated responses can be imperfect. Please do not rely on LiveAsk as a substitute for professional, legal, financial, medical or other specialist advice.

5. Voice conversations

When you choose voice, your speech is streamed in real time to OpenAI, our AI voice provider, which produces the spoken reply. LiveAsk does not save audio or create an audio recording. The final transcript of the voice interaction is added to the ordinary conversation record and kept for 90 days.

OpenAI may keep standard abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Under its commercial API terms, OpenAI does not use API content to train its models unless the customer opts in, and LiveAsk has not opted in.

6. Live calls with a person

A live call connects you with a person, not an AI. Live calls are not recorded and are not transcribed, and LiveAsk's AI does not listen to them.

Calls ordinarily use an encrypted direct connection between your device and the other person's device. Where a direct connection cannot be made, encrypted media may be relayed through Cloudflare's global network. A relay carries the encrypted call but cannot read it, and it sees connection details such as IP addresses, timing and traffic volume.

LiveAsk keeps basic call records for 90 days: that a call was offered and answered, the outcome, start and end times, duration and whether the connection was direct or relayed. The temporary call room itself expires after two hours.

7. Phone and email verification

To reduce false or mistyped contact details, LiveAsk may ask you to verify a phone number or email address with a code.

A phone number is sent to our SMS verification provider, currently Twilio, which may keep the destination phone number for up to 30 days. Email codes and other emails are sent through our email provider, currently Resend, which keeps email content and logs for 30 days.

LiveAsk's own pending verification records expire within minutes: 20 minutes for a phone code and 30 minutes for an email code.

If you give a phone number or email address as part of an enquiry, LiveAsk may use it to verify your details and to arrange the contact you asked for. LiveAsk does not currently use contact details collected through LiveAsk for unrelated marketing.

8. Who receives your information

  • On a website that uses LiveAsk: the business that runs that website receives your enquiry, your verified contact details and the conversation transcript, so it can follow up.
  • On a demonstration: the email address you verify as the owner receives the sample lead and any call link. If a Partner or LiveAsk has set a recipient for the demonstration, it goes to that recipient.
  • LiveAsk's operator, who may receive copies of conversations by email for monitoring, quality and safety.
  • Our service providers, as described in section 11.
  • Authorities or others where the law requires or permits.

We do not sell personal information.

9. Demonstrations

A LiveAsk demonstration is prepared by LiveAsk or by one of its Partners for a particular business. It is built mainly from information that business has published on its own website, together with details the preparer supplies. A business that did not ask for a demonstration can ask us to remove it (section 17).

A demonstration may include a recipient (name, email and optionally a mobile number) entered by the person who prepared it, who must have that person's consent. That recipient stays with the demonstration until it is edited or deleted.

Demonstration access links expire after 7 days. Details you enter during a demonstration session are held for that session and are not added to the demonstration's configuration.

Each demonstration page links back to LiveAsk. Following that link carries the demonstration and Partner identifiers described in section 12.

10. Customers and Partners

If you buy or sell LiveAsk services, we collect your contact and billing details, order and payment references, Partner settlement details and records of the terms you accepted. Where you pay by card through Stripe, Stripe handles the card details and LiveAsk does not receive your full card number. We keep these records as long as needed for accounts, tax, disputes and legal obligations.

11. Service providers and overseas processing

LiveAsk uses third-party providers to operate its services. Depending on the interaction, information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare, for website, application, storage and (where used) call relay infrastructure;
  • Anthropic, for text answers;
  • OpenAI, for voice;
  • Twilio, for phone verification;
  • Resend, for email delivery (provider data stored in the United States);
  • Stripe, where you pay for a LiveAsk service;
  • browser and device push-notification services, where a LiveAsk Exec user turns on notifications. These alerts are encrypted and show only generic text by default, such as that a visitor wants to speak.

Those providers run their own systems and privacy practices. Many are based or operate in the United States or elsewhere overseas, and some process data on a global network. We have not restricted our storage to a particular country, so information may be processed or stored in Australia and overseas. We do not promise that any information stays in Australia.

Anthropic says it ordinarily deletes commercial API inputs and outputs within 30 days and does not use them to train its models unless the customer permits it. LiveAsk has not given that permission.

13. Why we use information

We use information to:

  • run LiveAsk and answer visitor questions by text or voice;
  • connect live calls;
  • verify contact details and deliver enquiries and requested copies of conversations;
  • send sample leads and call links in demonstrations;
  • prevent fraud, abuse and misuse, and keep the platform secure;
  • troubleshoot problems and keep the service reliable;
  • monitor conversations for quality, safety and service improvement;
  • meter usage and costs;
  • measure demonstration and Partner attribution;
  • run our customer and Partner relationships, including billing and settlement;
  • carry out service administration and aggregate operational analysis; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use visitor information for advertising profiles or cross-site advertising, and we do not train our own AI models on it. We have not agreed to our providers using it to train their models.

15. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where appropriate. We may need enough detail to find the record and to check the request is yours. We aim to respond within 30 days.

A request to delete a conversation does not necessarily require deletion of a minimal administrative record that the request was received and handled, where keeping that record is reasonably required.

Where LiveAsk handled your information for a business whose website you used, that business may also hold it and you may need to ask the business as well.

16. Security, complaints and changes

Security. LiveAsk takes reasonable steps to protect information in the systems it operates. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a material privacy or security issue, we will assess and respond to it according to our obligations and the circumstances.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact us first (section 17) and we will look into it. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Third-party websites. The LiveAsk website may link to other websites. We do not control their privacy practices.

Changes. We may update this policy as our service, providers and legal requirements change. The effective date at the top shows the current version.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, complaints, or to ask us to remove a demonstration prepared for your business, email privacy@liveask.au.
